PS1-style asset packs by Cordatix

Modular kits, textures, props, monster and character packs, set pieces and anomaly kits for PS1-style horror and liminal games, built to one scale, metres on a 2 m grid, so kits, props and textures from different packs fit the same level. Every pack opens in Godot 4 with a PSX shader you can switch off, and in Blender as an asset library; the 3D packs add FBX for Unity and Unreal, plus GLB and OBJ.
